NOTAM Q-Code Legend

Example Q Line Anatomy

Q) CZYZ / QOLAS / IV / M / AE / 000 / 014 / 4447N07822W005

  • CZYZ: FIR (Toronto FIR)
  • QOLAS: Q + OL Subject + AS Condition
  • IV: IFR + VFR traffic
  • M: Miscellaneous purpose
  • AE: Aerodrome + En-route scope
  • 000/014: Surface to 1,400 ft
  • 4447N07822W005: Center + 5 NM radius
