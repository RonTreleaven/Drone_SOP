Pilot Defaults and Mission Category

Saved drone profile suggests category. Micro is planned at 250g or less. Basic is planned up to 25kg and at least 3 NM (5.6 km) from an airport center. Advanced is planned for 251g to 25kg and requires Advanced certification, registration proof, safety requirement compliance, and controlled-airspace permission when applicable.

Micro target: 250g. Basic/Advanced target: under 25kg (<25000g).
Micro Manual plan override
Advanced planning reminder: confirm Advanced Drone Pilot certification, registration proof, safety requirement compliance, and controlled-airspace permissions when operating in controlled airspace.

Emergency Quick Panel

  1. Stabilize aircraft and prioritize public safety.
  2. Land immediately when safe or execute controlled return-to-home.
  3. Confirm pilot and observer safety status.
  4. Document time, location, and incident details.
  5. Notify required authorities and internal contacts.
