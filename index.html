Drone SOP Mission Hub

Introduction

This Drone Mission Hub follows a 'Know Before You Go' approach with repeatable SOP checklists to consider NAV Canada NOTAMs, local Weather/Kp checks and a mission review for flight planning. The intent is to provide a simple, mobile friendy, and consistent workflow for drone operators to plan and execute their missions, while adhering to safety guidelines and regulations where required.

New to drone ops? Review Transport Canada: Flying Your Drone Safely and Legally.

Use at your own risk!

All information shared here is for personal use and in development. The Pilot in Command takes accountability and is responsible for all drone flight decisions and survey actions they trust.

To begin: Set your current Pilot Location, then select Tools, SOP Checklists, and complete Flight Logs to Save and keep records of your drone operation and due diligence.
