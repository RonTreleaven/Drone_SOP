Drone SOP Mission Hub

Introduction

This Drone Mission Hub is built around a Know Before You Go approach—giving you a streamlined, repeatable workflow to prepare for every flight.

Quickly review key factors like NOTAMs, local weather, Kp index, and airspace considerations, then step through a structured mission checklist designed for consistency and safety.

Optimized for mobile use, this hub helps drone operators plan and execute missions with confidence—while staying aligned with applicable regulations and best practices.

New to drone ops? Review Transport Canada: Flying Your Drone Safely and Legally.

⚠  Disclaimer – Use at your own risk

This tool is provided for informational and planning support only. It does not replace official sources such as NAV CANADA, nor does it constitute legal or operational authorization.

By proceeding, you acknowledge that:

  • You are responsible for verifying all flight information through official sources
  • You understand and will comply with applicable regulations
  • You assume full responsibility for the safe conduct of your drone operations
