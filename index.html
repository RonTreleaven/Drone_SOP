Drone SOP Mission Hub

Introduction Hide

Drone Mission Hub is built around a Know Before You Go philosophy, bringing the essential elements of pre-flight planning into a single, structured workflow.

Before every flight, Canadian drone pilots are responsible for gathering operational information and determining that the proposed flight can be conducted safely. This includes conducting a site survey and assessing the operational volume, taking into account applicable regulations, environmental conditions, nearby hazards, airspace restrictions, people, structures, and other factors relevant to the planned operation.

The Drone Mission Hub consolidates many of the resources needed to support that decision-making process. Review current NOTAMs, aviation weather, space weather, airspace information, and mission-specific considerations before working through a consistent pre-flight checklist designed to improve preparation, situational awareness, and operational safety.

Whether you're flying recreationally or commercially, the Hub promotes a repeatable mission-planning workflow that supports informed Go / No-Go decisions while helping reduce the chance of overlooked items. It is designed for both desktop and mobile devices, providing quick access to the information and tools needed before every mission.

New to drone operations? Begin by reviewing Transport Canada's Flying Your Drone Safely and Legally guide, together with the applicable requirements of Canadian Aviation Regulations (CARs) Part IX, including CAR 901.24 – Pre-flight Information and CAR 901.27 – Site Survey.
