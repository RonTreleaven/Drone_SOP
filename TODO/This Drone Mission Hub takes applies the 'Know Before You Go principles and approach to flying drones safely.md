This Drone Mission Hub takes applies the 'Know Before You Go" principles and approach to flying drones safely.  
This site is intended to aid drone pilots with repeatable checklists and workflows as standard operating procedures (SOP), in which to survey the area by location, fetch NOTAMs and tools to assess obstacles and air traffic warnings, perform local weather and planetary KP index checks, to help evaluate mission risk and best practices.

Just getting started?  See also Transport Canada's [Flying Your Drone Safely and Legally](https://tc.canada.ca/en/aviation/drone-safety/learn-rules-you-fly-your-drone/flying-your-drone-safely-legally?utm_campaign=tc-drone-safety-ongoing&utm_medium=vurl&utm_source=canada-ca-safe-legal-drones) web site for flying legally and safely.

To begin, set your current location for using Tools and Flight Log recording of your drone operations and mapping aids available here.

All information shared here, is for personal use and in no way implied to be 100%.  This project has been a hobby project for me.  Use at your own risk.
