Drone Flight Planning

[NAV CANADA Drone Flight Planning](https://www.navcanada.ca/en/flight-planning/drone-flight-planning.aspx)



It is important for all pilots - including RPAS operators (such as drones) - to operate safely in Canadian airspace. 

You must understand the regulations and take appropriate safety precautions before taking flight. This includes requesting NAV CANADA authorization to fly your RPAS in controlled airspace.



## Fly your drone safely and legally

Review important guidelines, discover where you can fly, schedule flights, and request permission to fly in airspace controlled by NAV CANADA all from the palm of your hand. Download NAV Drone on your mobile device, or use the web version.   There are many options to survey your location, NAV CAN resources needed to help fly safe.
