Standalone reference for pilots researching Q-code meanings.

Q Line Anatomy Example

Q) CZYZ / QOLAS / IV / M / AE / 000 / 014 / 4447N07822W005

  • CZYZ: FIR
  • QOLAS: Q + subject OL + condition AS
  • IV: IFR + VFR
  • M: Miscellaneous purpose
  • AE: Aerodrome + En-route
  • 000 / 014: Surface to 1,400 ft
  • 4447N07822W005: Center and radius
