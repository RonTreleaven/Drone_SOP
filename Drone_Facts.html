Facts About My Drone Flight

Attribution to Drone Pilot Assoc Canada and their Leaflet v4 - 2025 - "The Facts About My Drone Flight v-4"

[Drone Pilot Assoc Canada source ]

My drones are registered with Transport Canada where required, and I hold a current and valid RPA pilot certification, issued by Transport Canada.

Before Take-Off, I have considered:

  • Category of the airspace
  • Environmental conditions
  • Potential ground hazards
  • Proximity to people
  • Serviceability of my drone and its control equipment

I am flying my drone in full compliance with Canadian Aviation Regulations. I have reviewed the location and if required, obtained any necessary permissions or authorizations to operate here — including from Air Traffic Services (NAV CANADA) or designated authorities if in restricted airspace (Class F).

Questions or Concerns?

Please wait until the drone has landed before speaking to me. This ensures I can focus on flying safely. If you're still concerned, once we've had the opportunity to discuss, you may file a report with Transport Canada:

  • Drone Incident Report Form
