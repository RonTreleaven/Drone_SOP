Facts About My Drone Flight

DPAC Leaflet V-4

Note: Drones weighing less than 250g do not need to be registered with Transport Canada, and the pilot does not need to be certified.

My drone is registered with Transport Canada, and I hold an appropriate pilot certification issued by Transport Canada.

Before Take-Off, I Considered:

  • Category of the airspace
  • Environmental conditions
  • Potential ground hazards
  • Proximity to people
  • Serviceability of my drone and its control equipment

I am flying my drone in full compliance with Canadian Aviation Regulations.

I have reviewed the location and obtained any necessary permissions or authorizations to operate here — including from air traffic services (NAV CANADA) or designated authorities if in restricted airspace (Class F).

Questions or Concerns?

Please wait until the drone has landed before speaking to me. This ensures I can focus on flying safely.

If you're still concerned, you may file a report with Transport Canada:

  • Drone Incident Report Form
