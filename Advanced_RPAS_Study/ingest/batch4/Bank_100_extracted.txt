Validation basis
This question bank is written as a Canadian Advanced RPAS study resource. Questions have been normalized to the current regulatory framework rather than preserving superseded limits or legacy wording. Each item includes a keyed answer, a concise rationale, and a direct reference to an authoritative Canadian source.
Primary authority hierarchy: Canadian Aviation Regulations (Justice Laws) → Transport Canada standards (921/922) → Transport Canada TP 15263 → current TC AIM.
Important: where a topic historically used an older threshold or process, the question has been updated to the current rule. This is particularly relevant to SFOC categories, Level 1 Complex operations, DND aerodrome rules, declarations, and flight-reviewer requirements.

51. During which thunderstorm stage are mature convective hazards such as heavy precipitation, lightning, strong updrafts/downdrafts and turbulence most fully developed?

B. Mature stage
C. Dissipating stage
D. Stratus stage
Answer: B. The mature stage contains both updrafts and downdrafts and is normally the most hazardous phase.

52. A microburst is best described as:
A. A broad high-level jet stream

53. Atmospheric density is:
