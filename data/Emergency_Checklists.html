Non-Participant Enters Flight Area

·         Maintain visual contact with RPA

·         Communicate the situation to the Flight Crew

·         Verify

o   Check state of RPA (Status/Flight Mode)

o   Check location of RPA in flight area

·         Take Actions

o   Ground Crew – Alert flight area, remove non-participant

o   Visual Observer – Monitor air traffic

o   PIC – Pause flight operation and evaluate best course of action.

·         Issues

o   If non-participant is unable to be removed: 

§  Stop flight operation until the issue is resolved:

o   If non-participant blocks landing location:

§  Locate suitable divert location

·         Post-Incident

o   Document incident

o   Evaluate crowd control measures

 

 

Hazardous Weather Conditions

·         Maintain visual contact with RPA

·         Communicate the situation to the Flight Crew

·         Verify

o   Check state of RPA (Status/Flight Mode)

o   Check RPA location/altitude

·         Take Actions

o   Ground Crew – Review weather forecast

o   Visual Observer – Monitor changing weather conditions

o   PIC – Evaluate if conditions exceed safe operating requirements

·         Issues

o   If weather conditions are changing rapidly: 

§  Maneuver the RPA to a safe divert location

§  Land immediately

o   Err on the side of caution:

§  Maneuver the RPA closer to PIC to evaluate conditions

·         Post-Incident

o   Document incident

 

 

 

Low Battery or Status Error

·         Maintain visual contact with RPA

·         Communicate the situation to the Flight Crew

·         Verify

o   Check state of RPA (Status/Flight Mode)

o   Check location of RPA in flight area

·         Take Actions

o   Ground Crew – Alert flight area

o   Visual Observer – Monitor air traffic and contact ATC if necessary

o   PIC – Determine safest landing location. Land as soon as possible.

·         Issues

o   If disoriented: 

§  Use `Return to Home' to bring RPA back for landing

§  Regain visual contact with RPA as soon as possible

o   If strong winds prevent return flight:

§  Use camera to locate suitable divert location

·         Post-Incident

o   Document incident

o   Recovery telemetry logs

o   Inspect RPA and battery for physical signs of damage

 

Collision with Hazard

·         Maintain visual contact with RPA

·         Communicate the situation to the Flight Crew

·         Verify

o   Check state of RPA (Status/Flight Mode)

o   Check RPA location/altitude

·         Take Actions

o   Ground Crew – Alert and clear flight area, prepare safety equipment

o   Visual Observer – Guide PIC around hazard

o   PIC – Establish positive control and land immediately

·         Issues

o   If the RPA regains stability and control: 

§  Maneuver the RPA to a safe divert location

§  Land immediately

o   If the RPA does not regain stability:

§  Initiate manual motor shutdown

·         Post-Incident

o   Document incident

o   Inspect RPA and battery for physical signs of damage

 

Fly Away/Loss of GPS

·         Maintain visual contact with RPA

·         Communicate the situation to the Flight Crew

·         Verify

o   Check state of RPA (Status/Flight Mode)

o   Check RPA location/altitude

o   Check transmitter/tablet status and control links

·         Take Actions

o   Ground Crew – Alert and clear flight area, prepare safety equipment

o   Visual Observer – Monitor air traffic and contact ATC if necessary

o   PIC – Follow RPA and attempt to re-establish connection/positive control

·         Issues

o   If control is active, and RPA is drifting or moving incorrectly: 

§  Lower altitude and attempt to keep RPA stationary

§  Try ‘Return to Home’ to bring RPA back for landing

§  Try switching control to ‘manual’ or attitude control

o   If control is active but RPA is not responding:

§  Try switching control to ‘manual’ or attitude control

o   If control is not active

§  Assist Ground Crew in alerting and clearing flight area

·         Post-Incident

o   Document incident

o   Recovery telemetry logs

o   Inspect RPA and battery for physical signs of damage

 

Lost Link

·         Maintain visual contact with RPA

·         Communicate the situation to the Flight Crew

·         Verify

o   Check state of RPA (Status/Flight Mode)

o   Check RPA location/altitude

o   Check transmitter/tablet status and control links

·         Take Actions

o   Ground Crew – Alert and clear flight area, prepare safety equipment

o   Visual Observer – Monitor air traffic and contact ATC if necessary

o   PIC – Follow RPA and attempt to re-establish connection/positive control

·         Issues

o   If telemetry is active, but video feed has failed: 

§  Lower altitude and attempt to keep RPA stationary

§  Try ‘Return to Home’ to bring RPA back for landing

§  Try switching control to ‘manual’ or attitude control

o   If telemetry/control is not active

§  Try restarting controller/tablet

§  Adjust antennas and attempt to improve connection while following RPA

§  Assist Ground Crew in alerting and clearing flight area

·         Post-Incident

o   Document incident

o   Recovery telemetry logs

o   Inspect RPA and battery for physical signs of damage

 

In-Flight or Post-Flight Fire

·         Maintain visual contact with RPA

·         Communicate the situation to the Flight Crew

·         Verify

o   Check state of RPA (Status/Flight Mode)

o   Check RPA location/altitude

o   Check transmitter/tablet status and control links

·         Take Actions

o   Ground Crew – Alert and clear flight area, prepare safety equipment

o   Visual Observer – Be prepared to call emergency services

o   PIC – Immediately terminate flight

·         Issues

o   If RPA sparks a ground fire: 

§  Secure site and attempt to extinguish the fire with Ground Crew

o   If unable to extinguish fire:

§  Call emergency services

§  Stop all fire suppression efforts and begin minimizing potential damage by clearing the area and removing other potential fire hazards

o   DO NOT COMPROMISE YOUR SAFETY

·         Post-Incident

o   Document incident

o   Contact PI and Designated Local Authority

 

Pilot Incapacitation

·         Ensure the PIC is IMMEDIATELY attended to

o   Typically, the VO will be the closest person to the PIC

·         Communicate the situation to the Flight Crew

·         Take Actions

o   VO to pause or stop flight operation, then resume attending to PIC

o   Ground Crew must be prepared to call emergency services

o   Check transmitter/tablet status and control links

·         Issues

o   Call emergency services if there is a possibility that they may be needed

o   If flight operation cannot be paused or stopped, initiate a manual termination, unless an alternative operator is available.

o   RESPONSE PRIORITIES:

§  Avoid delaying emergency services to PIC

§  Ensure RPA will not place additional persons at risk

§  Prevent the RPA from causing property damage

§  If able, land the RPA safely

·         Post-Incident

o   Document incident

o   Contact PI and Designated Local Authority

 

 

Airspace Encroachment

·         Maintain visual contact with RPA

·         Communicate the situation to the Flight Crew

·         Verify

o   Check state of RPA (Status/Flight Mode)

o   Check location of RPA in flight area

·         Take Actions

o   Ground Crew – Alert flight area, prepare for landing

o   Visual Observer – Monitor air traffic and provide

o   PIC – Evaluate best course of action

·         Issues

o   If a collision is imminent: 

§  PIC must take immediate evasive action

o   If a collision is a strong possibility:

§  PIC must land or terminate the flight at the nearest divert or safe location

o   If the encroachment is unsafe but not likely to result in a collision:

§  PIC must pause flight, reduce flight altitude and monitor the situation.

§  PIC may only resume flight operation when the hazard has passed

o   If the encroachment is noticeable, but not likely to cause a safety issue:

§  PIC may continue flight operations

·         Post-Incident

o   Document incident

o   Review See & Avoid procedures
